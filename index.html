About Top Celebrities Award

Top Celebrities Award (TCA) is a recognition platform created to celebrate the personalities who have made a meaningful impact through talent, creativity, influence, entertainment, and their connection with the public — bringing fans, celebrities, artists, creators, and industry professionals together in one place dedicated to celebrating excellence.

TCA is registered under Artisticals, which provides the foundation through which the awards are organized and presented — building a credible platform for recognition that goes beyond popularity, spanning music, acting, comedy, content creation, media, fashion, and digital entertainment.

Fans are central to it. Through nominations and premium voting, the public gets a direct voice in deciding who's recognized. Voting is paid, which funds the platform, verification, event production, and everything else needed to run a professional awards experience — while giving every supporter a way to actively stand behind their favorite nominee.

The goal is an exciting, transparent, professional annual awards experience — one where established stars and emerging personalities alike receive real recognition, and where fans get a genuine voice in celebrating the people they support.
